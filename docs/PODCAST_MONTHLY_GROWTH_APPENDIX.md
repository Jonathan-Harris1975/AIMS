# Nonblocking podcast growth appendix in second-Friday Content Master audit

`audits/utils/contentMasterPipeline.js` uses the existing monthly run. A
read-only, deterministic appendix shows recent private podcast search samples and
the availability of genuine KDP royalty workbooks. No extra council seats,
model inference, third-party calls, new schedule or KDP writes.

Evidence is advisory. An API search result position is NOT a verified Apple
Podcasts chart rank or listener count. A KDP workbook is NOT interpreted as
sales until verified royalty parsing and reconciliation exist. Missing data
remains explicitly unknown, never zero. The appendix does not alter hard source
freshness, content-quality gates, existing RAMS dispatch, PodSEO Free or Headroom.

The weekly discovery collection schedule belongs to MAST (separate PR) and
must be merged only after the read-only AIMS growth endpoint is live.
