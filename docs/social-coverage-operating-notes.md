# Social coverage operating notes

_Last updated: 2026-05-30_

## LinkedIn

LinkedIn is not required for the current Jonathan Harris social automation setup. Do not add LinkedIn to the required OneUp network list or Blotato default channel list unless a future review explicitly changes the channel strategy.

## Weekend Blotato coverage

Do not add extra weekend Blotato publishing pressure at this point. Weekend video coverage should be monitored against available Blotato credits, output quality, and platform performance before any move towards additional Saturday or Sunday automation.

The current operating decision is to watch credit usage and consider a different future approach if weekend video becomes worthwhile. Options could include selective manual runs, lower-frequency weekend shorts, or repurposed static/social-blog material rather than fully automatic extra credit spend.
