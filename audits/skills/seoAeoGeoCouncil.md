# SEO/AEO/GEO Council Skill

## Purpose
A standing monthly council that receives the fresh SEO/AEO/GEO forensic audit and turns it into a RAMS-readable master decision report.

The council must separate search/content recommendations from repo patch instructions. It must identify source ownership before recommending action.

## Council members

- SEO/AEO/GEO Lead — owns search visibility, answer-engine readiness, GEO citation quality and JH-* issue movement.
- Route & Coverage Lead — owns failed URLs, legacy routes, redirects, canonical evidence and coverage state.
- Workbook & Source Governance Lead — owns workbook, repo, sitemap, RSS/feed and manifest alignment.
- R2 & Podcast Source Ownership Lead — owns R2-hosted podcast/blog/transcript route classification and prevents website-repo patch leakage.
- Transcript & Episode Quality Lead — owns transcript summaries, takeaways, entity indexes, anchors and schema alignment.
- Schema & Structured Data Lead — owns JSON-LD coverage, schema type correctness and visible-content alignment.
- Content Architecture Lead — owns topic hubs, glossary, blog structure, question-led headings and internal linking.
- Internal Linking & Conversion Path Lead — owns book/topic/podcast/newsletter connections and commercial search paths.
- eBook Discovery & Commercial Intent Lead — owns whether search, AEO snippets and topic pages lead readers clearly towards relevant Jonathan Harris ebooks without hype.
- Automation Safety Lead — owns RAMS source ownership, deterministic evidence, patch eligibility and manual-review boundaries.

## RAMS rules

- Prefer this council report over the raw SEO/AEO/GEO report.
- Auto-patch only when a finding has exact website-owned affectedPaths, deterministic evidence, an approved fix class and a clear verification method.
- R2 podcast, R2 blog and transcript generator findings must be manual review or generator tasks, not website repo patches.
- Aggregate family findings are manual review until converted into deterministic source-level findings.
